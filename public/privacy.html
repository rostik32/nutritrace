Privacy Policy

NutriTrace · Last updated: 27 October 2026

NutriTrace is a personal food, nutrition and fitness diary. It is run by the diaitika project and is available at https://nutri.diaitika.com. This policy explains what data the service handles, why, and what choices you have. Contact us at help@diaitika.com.

1. Who we are

The operator of this service is the diaitika project (contact: help@diaitika.com). NutriTrace runs on a server that the operator controls. The service is not offered to the public as a commercial product, and it is not a medical device.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: username, a password stored only as a salted hash, and optional details you add (full name, nickname, birthday, gender, email address).
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, your email address and your name. We never receive your Google password.
  • Food and diary data: foods, meals, recipes, diary entries, quantities, water intake, goals and settings.
  • Body and activity data: body measurements such as weight, workouts, fasting and activity logs. This can be health-related data.
  • Wearable data (only if you connect a device): data that you allow us to read from Fitbit, Withings, Garmin or Google Health. You can disconnect a device at any time in the app.
  • Photos you upload, for example pictures of meals.
  • AI assistant data (only if you use the assistant): the messages you send and the chat history.
  • Technical data: server logs with request times and paths, and your IP address, which we use for security and to limit repeated requests.

3. How we use data

We use your data to provide the diary and its features, to sign you in, to send emails you asked for (password reset, invitations, notices that someone shared an item with you), to keep the service secure and to fix problems. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. Google user data

NutriTrace uses information received from Google APIs only to sign you in and to link your account, and, if you connect Google Health, to read the health data you authorise. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

5. Third-party services

Some features send data to outside services. They are used only when you use the related feature or the operator has configured it:

  • Google – sign-in and, if you choose, Google Health.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central – food search. Search terms are sent to them.
  • Fitbit, Withings, Garmin – wearable data you connect.
  • An AI provider you configure (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini) – the assistant's messages and the food data it needs are sent there.
  • Brevo – sends our service emails.
  • Cloudflare – network protection and secure connection to our server. Your traffic passes through it.
  • Oracle Cloud – hosts the server.

Those providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies

We use only a strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Storage, backups and retention

Your data is stored in the service's database on the server. The operator keeps copies of the database and uploaded photos as backups in the operator's Google Drive for 30 days, and then they are deleted. Data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your data is deleted from the database. Copies in backups are removed when the backups expire.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct and export your data from the app (export options in the app's settings).
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account).
  • Disconnect Google sign-in or a wearable at any time. You can also remove NutriTrace's access in your Google Account permissions.
  • Ask us a question or make a request at help@diaitika.com. We answer within a reasonable time.

9. Children

NutriTrace is not intended for children under 16. Do not create an account for a child under that age.

10. Health information

NutriTrace is a tracking tool. It does not give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or a dietitian.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
